Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how JOYET may collect, use, store, disclose and protect information when you use the JOYET website, application and related services.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 Version 1.0

Privacy matters

JOYET is designed to collect information needed to provide and secure payment, account and digital-service functionality. We aim to collect only what is reasonably necessary for those purposes.

For purposes of this Privacy Policy, "JOYET", "we", "us" and "our" refer to the entity responsible for operating the JOYET services. The exact legal entity and applicable jurisdiction should be identified in the final published version of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use JOYET, we may collect information that is necessary to create and secure your account, process deposits and transactions, provide requested services, prevent abuse and meet applicable legal or provider requirements.

Information you provide

  • Name and basic account information
  • Email address and contact details
  • Account credentials and authentication information
  • Information you provide when contacting support
  • Information required for applicable identity or verification procedures

Information generated through your use of JOYET

  • Account activity
  • Deposit and transaction information
  • Service purchases and payment activity
  • Login and authentication events
  • Security and fraud-prevention signals

2. Account Information

When you create or use a JOYET account, we may collect information needed to establish and maintain that account.

Depending on the services you use, we may also require additional information to verify your identity, comply with applicable legal requirements, manage account security or satisfy requirements imposed by service providers.

You are responsible for ensuring that information provided to JOYET is accurate and current.

3. Deposits and Transaction Information

When you deposit supported cryptocurrency into JOYET, we may process information relating to the transaction, such as the cryptocurrency asset, blockchain network, transaction identifier, deposit address, timestamp, amount and confirmation status.

Blockchain transactions can contain information that is publicly visible on the relevant network. Public blockchain information may be available independently of JOYET and may not be removable from the blockchain.

For outgoing transfers and digital services, we may process information such as recipient details, destination account information, transaction amounts, references, service type, transaction status and related records.

4. Device and Technical Information

When you access JOYET, certain technical information may be collected automatically, depending on the device, browser, operating system and configuration you use.

This may include:

  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system
  • Browser or application information
  • Language and general regional settings
  • Login timestamps and connection information
  • Crash, diagnostics and performance information

5. How We Use Information

We may use information we collect for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing your JOYET account
  • Processing crypto deposits
  • Maintaining your JOYET balance
  • Processing transfers to supported destinations
  • Providing airtime, data, electricity, cable and other supported services
  • Providing customer support
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity
  • Securing our systems and services
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations
  • Improving the functionality and reliability of JOYET
  • Communicating important account, service or security information

6. When We Share Information

We do not share personal information indiscriminately. Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, protect JOYET and its users, or satisfy legal obligations.

Depending on the service you use, information may be shared with:

  • Payment and financial service providers
  • Cryptocurrency and blockchain infrastructure providers
  • Airtime and mobile-data providers
  • Electricity and utility providers
  • Cable or television service providers
  • Technology and infrastructure providers
  • Security, fraud-prevention and verification providers

We may also disclose information where required by law, regulation, court order, legal process or other valid governmental request.

7. Third-Party Service Providers

JOYET may rely on third-party providers to deliver certain parts of the platform. These providers may process information on our behalf or independently process information in connection with the services they provide.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and terms. Where appropriate, we encourage users to review the policies governing services they access through JOYET.

8. Information You Share With Other Users

Some JOYET functionality may involve transferring money to another JOYET user or interacting with another account.

Information required to identify or complete a transaction may therefore be visible to the relevant recipient or otherwise included in transaction records.

You should avoid including unnecessary personal or sensitive information in transfer notes or other optional transaction fields.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information and transaction data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Security measures may include access controls, encryption or protected transmission, monitoring, authentication controls and other safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved.

However, no system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are also responsible for keeping your device, account credentials and authentication methods protected.

10. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain transaction records, protect against fraud, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and comply with legal, regulatory, accounting or reporting requirements.

Different categories of information may therefore be retained for different periods.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information. These may include rights to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where legally available
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a copy of certain information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

These rights may be limited by legal obligations, security requirements, transaction-record requirements or other applicable exceptions.

12. Account Closure

If you close your JOYET account, we may retain certain information where required or reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, accounting or transaction-record purposes.

Closing an account does not necessarily result in immediate deletion of every record associated with that account.

13. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The JOYET website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, maintain sessions, understand site usage, improve performance and support security.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Restricting certain cookies may affect how parts of the website function.

14. Children's Privacy

JOYET is not intended for individuals who are not legally permitted to use the applicable services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to JOYET in circumstances where collection was not permitted, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

15. International Data Processing

Depending on the infrastructure and service providers used by JOYET, personal information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be considered or implemented for international transfers of personal information.

16. Third-Party Links

JOYET may contain links to third-party websites, applications or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.

You should review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing them with personal information.

17. Marketing Communications

Where permitted by applicable law, we may send service announcements, account notices and other communications relevant to your use of JOYET.

Marketing communications may provide an option to unsubscribe or manage communication preferences. Certain operational, security or transaction-related communications may still be sent when necessary to provide the service.

18. Data Accuracy

You are responsible for keeping the information associated with your JOYET account accurate and current.

If information changes or you discover an error in your account details, you should update the information or contact JOYET support where self-service changes are unavailable.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, legal obligations or privacy practices.

When changes are material, we may provide notice through the JOYET application, website or another appropriate channel.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently revised.

20. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to exercise an applicable privacy right, or have a concern about how your information is handled, please contact JOYET through the official contact information published on our website or within the JOYET application.
