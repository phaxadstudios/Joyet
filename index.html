Questions, answered.

Common questions about the Joyet platform.

Joyet is a crypto-powered payments and digital services platform designed to let users deposit supported crypto and use their Joyet balance for transfers and supported services.
You can deposit supported crypto by sending the asset to the appropriate Joyet deposit address for that asset and network.
Depending on the services available, your Joyet balance can be used for supported transfers, airtime, mobile data, electricity, cable TV and other digital services.
No. Wallet infrastructure can provide the crypto funding layer, while the broader Joyet platform is focused on transfers, payments and digital services.
Joyet is designed to support VTU services such as airtime and mobile data through supported service providers.
Supported assets and networks depend on Joyet's active wallet and payment infrastructure. The supported asset and network should always be confirmed before making a deposit.
