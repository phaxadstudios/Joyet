FAQ

Questions, answered.

A few things people may want to know before using Joyet.

Joyet is a crypto-powered payment and digital services platform. Users can deposit supported crypto and use their Joyet balance for supported transfers and everyday services.
You fund your Joyet account by depositing supported crypto assets into the appropriate Joyet deposit address.
Depending on the services available, Joyet can support transfers, airtime, mobile data, electricity, cable TV and other digital services.
Joyet can provide wallet infrastructure for supported crypto assets, but the broader product is designed around using crypto for payments, transfers and digital services.
Joyet is designed to support transfers to supported recipients and destinations. Availability depends on the implemented transfer infrastructure.
Supported assets and networks depend on Joyet's active wallet and payment infrastructure. The platform will clearly identify available assets before deposit.
