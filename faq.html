Getting started

Start with JOYET

JOYET is a crypto-funded payment and digital-services platform. You can deposit supported crypto, receive a JOYET balance, and use that balance for transfers and supported everyday services.

No. JOYET is built for payments and digital services. It is not an investment product and does not promise returns on deposited funds.

You can use your JOYET balance for supported bank transfers, transfers to other JOYET users, airtime, mobile data, electricity, cable TV and other available digital services.

Crypto deposits

Deposit crypto into JOYET

Select the deposit option in the JOYET app, choose a supported asset and network, then send the crypto from your exchange or personal wallet to the deposit details shown by JOYET.

Yes, when the asset and network are supported. You can withdraw supported crypto from an exchange such as Binance or Bybit to your JOYET deposit details.

Supported assets and networks will be shown inside the JOYET app. Always confirm both the asset and network before sending a deposit.

Crypto transactions can be difficult or impossible to reverse. Always verify the asset, network and deposit details shown by JOYET before confirming the transfer.

Payments

Move your JOYET balance

Yes. Supported bank transfers are part of the JOYET experience. Available banks, limits, fees and processing can depend on the payment services available in your region.

Yes. JOYET is designed to allow supported transfers between JOYET users using your available balance.

Applicable fees can vary by transaction and service. Any applicable fee should be shown before you confirm the transaction.

Security

Protect your account

JOYET is designed with account, transaction and infrastructure security controls. No online service can eliminate every risk, so users should also protect their login details and security codes.

No. Sending supported crypto from an exchange to your JOYET deposit details does not require you to provide JOYET with your exchange password.

Secure your account, stop suspicious activity where possible, and contact JOYET through an official support channel. Never share your password, OTP, PIN or recovery information with anyone claiming to be JOYET support.
