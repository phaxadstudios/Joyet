Getting started

Start with JOYET

JOYET is a crypto-funded payment and digital-services platform. Deposit supported crypto, receive a JOYET balance, and use that balance for transfers and supported everyday services.

No. JOYET is built for payments and digital services. It is not an investment product and does not promise returns on deposited funds.

Depending on availability, you can use your balance to send money to bank accounts, transfer money to other JOYET users, purchase airtime and data, pay supported electricity and cable bills, and use other digital services.

Crypto deposits

Deposit crypto into JOYET

Select the deposit option in the JOYET app, choose a supported crypto asset and network, then send the crypto from your exchange or personal wallet to the deposit details shown by JOYET. Once processed, the applicable amount is reflected in your JOYET balance.

Yes, when the asset and network are supported. You can withdraw supported crypto from an exchange such as Binance or Bybit to your JOYET deposit details. JOYET does not need your exchange password.

The supported assets and networks will be displayed inside the JOYET app. Always verify the selected asset and network before sending a deposit.

Crypto transactions can be difficult or impossible to reverse. Always confirm the asset, network and deposit details shown in JOYET before sending.

Timing can depend on the blockchain network, confirmations and JOYET's processing systems. The app will show the deposit status where available.

Payments

Move and use your balance

Yes. Supported bank transfers are part of the JOYET experience. Available banks, limits, fees and processing times depend on the applicable payment services available in your region.

Yes. JOYET is designed to make transfers between users simple using your available JOYET balance.

Fees can vary depending on the transaction and service. Any applicable fee should be shown before you confirm a transaction.

Security

Protect your account

JOYET is designed with account, transaction and infrastructure security controls. No online service can eliminate every possible risk, so users should also protect their login details and security codes.

No. Sending a supported crypto deposit from an exchange to your JOYET deposit details does not require you to provide JOYET with your exchange password.

Secure your account, stop suspicious activity where possible, and contact JOYET through an official support channel. Never share your password, OTP, PIN or recovery information with someone claiming to be support.
