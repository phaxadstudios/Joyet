General

Getting started with JOYET

JOYET is a crypto-funded payment and digital-services platform. You can deposit supported crypto, receive the corresponding JOYET balance, and use that balance for transfers and everyday services.

Your JOYET balance can be used for supported bank transfers, transfers to other JOYET users, airtime, mobile data, electricity, cable TV and other supported digital services.

No. JOYET is designed as a payment and digital-services platform. It is not presented as an investment product and does not promise returns on deposited funds.

Crypto deposits

Turning crypto into JOYET balance

JOYET is designed around crypto funding. You select a supported asset inside the app, receive the appropriate deposit details, and send the crypto from your exchange or personal wallet. Once the deposit is processed, your JOYET balance is updated according to the applicable conversion and processing rules.

Yes, where the asset and network are supported. You can send crypto from an exchange such as Binance or Bybit to the deposit details displayed by JOYET. JOYET does not require your exchange password or exchange account credentials.

Supported assets and networks will be listed directly inside the JOYET app. Always confirm both the asset and network before sending a deposit.

Processing time can depend on the blockchain network, confirmation requirements and JOYET's processing systems. The app will show the status of your deposit where available.

Crypto transactions can be irreversible. Always check the asset, network and deposit details shown by JOYET before confirming a transaction. Recovery may not be possible for unsupported or incorrectly sent assets.

Security

Keeping your account protected

JOYET is designed with account, transaction and infrastructure security controls. However, no online service can eliminate every possible risk. You should also protect your login details and never share security codes with anyone.

No. JOYET does not need your exchange login credentials simply for you to send a supported crypto deposit to your JOYET deposit address.

Secure your account immediately, stop any suspicious activity, and contact JOYET through the official support channel. Never share your password, PIN, OTP or recovery information with someone claiming to be support.
