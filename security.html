Security layers

Security across every important step.

JOYET's architecture separates the major parts of the experience so account access, deposits, balances and outgoing transactions can each have appropriate controls.

Account security

Account authentication and access controls are designed to help protect your JOYET account from unauthorized use.

Transaction controls

Transaction flows are designed to make important details visible before users confirm a payment.

Crypto deposit safety

Deposit flows can display the relevant asset and network information before users send cryptocurrency to JOYET.

Privacy

Personal information should only be collected and processed for appropriate account, transaction, security and service purposes.

Activity monitoring

Security systems can monitor relevant activity and signals to identify suspicious or unusual behavior.

Provider security

External providers may be involved in payments, blockchain infrastructure and VTU services, so provider controls are an important part of the overall ecosystem.

Your role

Security works both ways.

The platform can provide security controls, but your account and device also play an important role.

No service can eliminate every risk

Security is an ongoing process. JOYET can use technical and organizational safeguards, but users should also protect their accounts and devices. Blockchain networks, financial providers, telecommunications providers and other third parties can also introduce risks outside JOYET's direct control.
